# GigaRaid

## Web Game Concept

A 2.5d game with sprite assets where users build up their village with in game tokens/coins to build up their defenses and villagers. The games concept is about the human natural instinct of survival and expansion. Every village has a main townhall that can be upgraded from level 1-10. The most previous resource in this universe is bitcoin. Players buy mines and miners from app store to mine bitcoin. You’re mined bitcoin is hoarded in storage and protected by your army.  When a user is away from their base, the pvp system built into the game can allow other villages to find said user and attack their base to take their resources. User should ideally not lose all their resources after every attack - maybe a fixed percentage. 

### Social Heirarchy

- Village - Every player starts off with a small village. You name your village and build it up.
- Clan - Villages can come together and form clans where they can communicate, coordinate and attack other clans. I can add chat and voice here

### Assets

- Buildings/land
    - Townhall - every player starts out with a lvl 1 townhall. The more you level up, the more items you unlock
    - Trencher Huts - this is where the builders sleep and recover
    - Defence Towers - Archers use this tower to defend the base
    - Bitcoin mines - this is the most precious resource in-game
    - Army camps - Where your army stays on your base
    - Storage - This is where loot and mined resources are stored
    - Elixir - magic potions for your army and to make spells
- Army/Troops
    - Minions - weakest army in the game, every player starts out with 10
    - Archers - self explanatory. Pack a punch but die easily
    - Giants - Big tall and strongest troop in the game but very slow
    - Bull riders - They sit on a bull and raid. Very effective, cause a lot of damage fast
    - Bears - These are a almost like pets that are hired weapons. Inflict a lot of damage and hard to kill only behind the giants.
    - Wizard - fire throwing demons that pack a lot of punch.
- Weapons
    - Canons
    - Land Bows

### Gameplay

Attacking and defending are all automatic. Each assets should be programmed to look for certain things. Army looks to destroy the entire base while defenses look to stop them. In this sense it is more of a strategy game. The player will have to strategically place their troops around the base and they will attack whatever is closest to them. Archers and wizards have a range and don’t need to get too close to attack. Same goes with tower defenses. Game match/raid logic should only match players of the same level. Main level is determined by the townhall lvl because this is what gives players the ability to unlock certain assets in the game. Players can buy in-game tokens with real money through the crypto native integrations with the robinhood chain. 

### Spells

- Damage spells - weakens opponents
- Healing spells - heals your army in battle when they’re taking damage
- Utility - gives you an advantage during battle; like temporily freezing the opponent armies, defenses etc.

### Interface

2.5D terrains , user can buy different terrains to customzie their village look. They can also customize their army’s look fit their aesthetic. Game will use sprite assets files for characters and setup to give a modern clean aesthetic.
